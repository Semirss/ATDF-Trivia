Q: Which planet is known as the red planet?
A: Mars
B: Venus
C: Jupiter
D: Mercury
Answer: A
Difficulty: Easy
Explanation: Mars is called the red planet because iron oxide on its surface gives it a reddish color.

Q: Which statement best explains why tides happen?
A: Winds push ocean water toward shore
B: Earth's rotation heats ocean currents
C: Gravity from the Moon and Sun pulls on ocean water
D: Rainfall raises and lowers sea levels
Answer: C
Difficulty: Medium
Explanation: Tides are mainly caused by the Moon's gravity, with help from the Sun's gravity.

Q: In a circuit with fixed voltage, what happens to current if resistance doubles?
A: It doubles
B: It is halved
C: It stays the same
D: It becomes zero
Answer: B
Difficulty: Hard
Explanation: Ohm's law says current equals voltage divided by resistance, so doubling resistance halves current.
